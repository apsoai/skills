Design the data model for subscription billing in a B2B SaaS. Organizations subscribe to plans. A plan's monthly price and its usage limits change over time. Existing subscribers keep the price and limits they signed up with until they renew, and finance needs to know which terms were in effect for any subscription on any past date. Write the result to `.apsorc` in the current directory, then print the final file contents and list anything the schema cannot enforce on its own.
